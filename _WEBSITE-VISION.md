## 1. Positioning + One-Line Vision

**The core repositioning is sound regardless of the credential audit:** Diligent Services is not a "smart-home installer" or a "general contractor." The current site's "20 years of general contracting / construction, renovation, and consulting" framing makes DS look like a remodeler with smart-home as a side gig — the opposite of the truth, and exactly the generalist perception to kill.

**Pick a hero lane: luxury residential.** Casa Carmelita (Sam's own 22-zone Savant/Lutron estate) and the named platforms anchor this credibly. The builder-program relationship is the *proof of repeatable, serviceable delivery*; commercial is opportunistic.

**A strategist's caution on "we span three markets."** The draft celebrated the three-market spread (bespoke residential + builder + commercial) as *the* differentiator. From a positioning chair, breadth usually reads as "we'll take any job" — the generalist perception we're trying to escape, and luxury buyers want a specialist. Using a *nightclub proposal* to prove range to a $500k-living-room homeowner can actively hurt the residential pitch. **Recommendation: do not elevate "we do all three" to the differentiator.** If the spread stays in the story, frame it as **"disciplined, serviceable delivery at any scale"** — repeatability, not range for its own sake. The genuinely defensible, least-replicable asset is the **builder-program** lane (multi-home, CO-bearing); give it disproportionate weight *if permissioned*, not equal billing as one of three.

**The throughline — conditional on the audit.** *If* the credential audit confirms Lutron/Ketra standing, **light** is the strongest spine (Lutron HomeWorks/QSX + Ketra tunable white, a real and scarce credential), then **control** (Savant/Crestron), then AV/networking/shades/security. *If it does not*, lead with **control & experience** — "we design and program the systems a home runs on" — and treat Lutron as a platform DS specifies expertly rather than a badge DS wears.

> ### One-line vision (recommended — credential-neutral, safe under either audit outcome)
> **"Diligent Services designs, programs, and maintains the lighting, control, and entertainment systems that make Southern California's finest homes feel effortless."**
>
> Hero tagline (short): **"Light. Control. Effortless living."**

**Voice:** calm, precise, understated-premium, technically fluent but human. Use *systems, light, experience, effortless, reliable, we program it / we maintain it.* Avoid *smart-home, gadgets, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art,* exclamation points. Let named platforms and **verified** real projects carry credibility instead of adjectives.

**On the name:** "Diligent Services" reads generic and collides with IT firms in search. Don't rename — but always pair it with the category in the wordmark (**"Diligent Services — Lighting & Control Integration"**) so it self-disambiguates everywhere.

## 3. Visual Direction

**Register: "quiet luxury."** Premium comes from restraint and whitespace, not effects — the brand whose technology you never see but always feel.

- **Color:** near-black / deep-charcoal canvas (#0E0F12 / #14161A), **warm** off-white text (#F4F1EA, not pure white), and ONE warm metallic accent — brushed brass/amber (~#C9A24B) for CTAs, rules, active states, kept under ~5% of any view. The warmth nods to tunable warm-to-cool lighting.
  - **[New — accessibility, was missing]** Specify **WCAG 2.1 AA contrast as a hard constraint.** Dark-luxury palettes routinely fail it; a brass accent under 5% must still clear 4.5:1 (or 3:1 for large text / UI) against the charcoal on every CTA and link. Audit contrast before launch, not after.
- **Typography:** high-contrast editorial serif for display (Canela / Ivar / GT Sectra) + clean neutral grotesque for body/UI (Söhne / Suisse, or Inter as the safe web font). Big headlines, generous leading, lots of negative space.
- **Imagery — the make-or-break variable:** photography IS the product. Full-bleed, low-key, warm-lit interiors where you photograph **the light and the architecture, not the gear.** Shoot real DS projects; never stock that screams smart-home. **Across every premium reference, thin imagery is where the positioning collapses — a sparse-but-stunning gallery beats a full-but-generic one.** (Reconciled with the photography risk in Sections 7–8.)
- **Hero:** single full-viewport cinematic image, or a slow 6–10s ambient loop of a lighting scene shifting warm-to-cool, with one serif positioning line and one quiet CTA. No carousels, no bullet clutter.
- **Motion:** restrained and physical — fades, slow parallax, viewport-entry reveals with long soft easing that feels like a motorized shade gliding. No bounce, no spin. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **[Critique correction — dropped]** The draft suggested matching the *internal ops dashboard* palette "so site and product feel like one brand." The dashboard is owner-gated internal tooling; a public marketing site matching an internal console is a non-goal. Build the brand palette for the *audience*, not for internal consistency.

**Cited references:**
- **audiovisions.com/napa-valley** — "Discreet technology. Effortless control." hero economy; invisible-tech imagery; dark canvas + warm accent.
- **wipliance.com** — text-forward heroes with heavy whitespace; black/cream + single accent; modular 3-column capability grid; the trust-signal stack (manufacturer badges, awards, named case studies); regional offices + phone.
- **bravas.com** — best-in-class **named-pillar framework** (Light & Shade, Sound & Vision); aspirational hero ("Live in harmony with your home").
- **savant.com** — design-language north star: refined minimal, dark accents, generous whitespace, experience-over-specs.
- **lutron.com / Ketra marketing** — how to photograph *light itself* (warm-to-cool dusk scenes) as the premium subject.

## 6. Technical Build + Hosting

**Ground truth:** the live site is a **Quarto 1.4.555 static site on nginx/1.24.0 (Hetzner, 95.217.133.86)**, Namecheap DNS, auto-renewing Let's Encrypt cert (valid through 2026-08-04), with robots.txt + sitemap. It is **not** hand-built HTML and **not** Cloudflare-fronted. Confirmed: `/contact.html` → 200, `/support` and `/contact` → 404, no custom 404. The real defects are stale identity copy and missing compliance surfaces — a rebuild is as much a *repositioning* as a tech swap.

**Apple compliance — corrected and expanded.** The draft framed the Apple flag as "the legal entity must be *visible on the website*," solvable with a footer line. **That overstates what the footer achieves.** Apple's actual requirements: enrollment is under the **legal entity (Carmelita Enterprises Inc); DBAs are not accepted**; the org's **D-U-N-S number must match the legal name**; and the **website's domain must be associated with the organization** (checked via registration/WHOIS and contact email). So:
- The **footer legal line + live `/support` mailto** is the part the *website* controls — necessary, ship it.
- **[Open question, possibly blocking] D-U-N-S:** does Carmelita Enterprises Inc have a D-U-N-S record matching the legal name? If not, no footer copy fixes the flag.
- **[Open question, possibly blocking] Domain registrant:** is diligentservices.io registered to Carmelita Enterprises Inc (or will Apple accept the association)? A personal registrant may not satisfy "associate your domain with your org."
- Note: a `/privacy` policy is good practice but is **not** an Apple org-verification requirement — don't conflate compliance surfaces.

**Stack recommendation:**
- **SSG: Astro.** The genuine 2025/26 default for image-heavy marketing/portfolio sites under ~200 pages: zero-JS-by-default (fast, strong local SEO), built-in image optimization, deep agency/portfolio theme ecosystem, component model + Git/PR workflow that already fits how DS works. **Honest reasoning (critique correction):** the win over Quarto is *developer ergonomics + image pipeline + theme ecosystem* — **not** that "Quarto can't do it" (Quarto supports multi-level nav and custom themes). Since Phase 0 *keeps* Quarto, don't trash it in the same doc. A CMS (WordPress/Webflow) adds attack surface, cost, and editor headcount DS doesn't have; copy changes are infrequent.
- **Hosting: keep nginx on the existing Hetzner box.** Do NOT move to Cloudflare Pages or front with CF now — DNS is at Namecheap (a move means an NS migration), the cert auto-renews, and a brochure site at DS volume gains nothing from edge CDN/DDoS. Keeping nginx also keeps the public site **independent of the dashboard's owner-gated CF Access stack** (which must never gate the public marketing site). Revisit CF only if traffic/DDoS ever justifies it.
- **Deploy: GitHub Actions building Astro → rsync-over-SSH to the nginx webroot.** Dedicated webroot-scoped deploy key in repo Secrets, password auth off, host key pinned. The current flow appears manual (homepage Last-Modified 2025-01-29, unchanged ~17 months), so CI deploy is a real improvement, not just parity.
